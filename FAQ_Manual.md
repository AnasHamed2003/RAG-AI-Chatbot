### How does CEA verification work?

**CEA Verification Process:**

🛡️ **Steps:**

1. Register with your CEA number
2. Admin team verifies via CEA Public Register
3. Verification takes 1-3 business days
4. Email notification upon completion

📝 **Format:**

• Typical format: R123456A or RB123456
• Enter exactly as on license
• Auto-formatted to uppercase

⏳ **While Pending:**

• Complete profile setup
• Explore dashboard
• Cannot generate codes until approved

Why manual? CEA has no public API for automated checks.
