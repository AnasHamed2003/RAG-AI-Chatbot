Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a field of computer science that aims to create machines capable of intelligent behavior. AI systems can learn from data, recognize patterns, make decisions, and solve problems.

Machine Learning is a subset of AI that focuses on algorithms that can learn from and make predictions on data. Deep Learning uses neural networks with many layers to model complex patterns.

Natural Language Processing (NLP) allows computers to understand and generate human language. This enables chatbots, translation services, and text analysis.

Computer Vision enables machines to interpret and understand visual information from the world, such as images and videos.

AI has applications in healthcare, finance, transportation, entertainment, and many other fields.
